I got tired of pasting DOM snippets, so I built a tool that uses video to generate Playwright scripts. If you are anything like us, your browser history is likely a graveyard of Gemini conversations and ChatGPT threads named "Fix this Playwright Selector" or "Generate Test Script." We have all tried using ChatGPT for testing. It usually goes like this: you open the dev tools, copy a massive chunk of your DOM, paste it into ChatGPT, and pray it understands which <button> is actually the "Submit" button. It works, but it is exhausting.
