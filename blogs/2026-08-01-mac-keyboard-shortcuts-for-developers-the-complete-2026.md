tldr: Mac keyboard shortcuts look like sixty unrelated key combinations. Most of them belong to one system that macOS borrowed from Emacs decades ago, which is why Control-A jumps to the start of a line in your editor, your terminal, Slack, and the Safari address bar. Learn that system, learn the dozen shortcuts that actually recover time, then build your own for whatever is left.
