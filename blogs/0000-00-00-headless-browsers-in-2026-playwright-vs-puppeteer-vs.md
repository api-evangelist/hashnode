tldr: Playwright wins new projects. Puppeteer wins Chromium-only tooling. Selenium wins legacy QA matrices. And somewhere around 20 parallel sessions, none of them should be running on your laptop. The question most teams get wrong Teams obsess over which automation library to pick. Playwright, Puppeteer, or Selenium. They argue about it in design docs. The library is the easy part. The hard part is where the browser actually runs. Local Chromium works for 1 developer, 5 scripts, and a weekend project.
