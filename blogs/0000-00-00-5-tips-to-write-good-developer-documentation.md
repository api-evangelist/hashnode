All developer tools go through five stages: discover, evaluate, learn, build, and scale. While you are in the discovery stage and moving towards the evaluation phase, this is where you should maximize focus on your docs. As they say, Documentation is the product. Your aim should be to have a great developer experience and to reduce the time to hello world (TTHW) to five minutes or less. When developers discover your tool, you only have a few minutes to get their attention.
