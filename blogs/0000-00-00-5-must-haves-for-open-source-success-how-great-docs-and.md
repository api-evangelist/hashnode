Building and maintaining an open-source project is hard. It is even harder when you are a solo maintainer or have a very small team. This article includes 5 top tips that you can follow to have a successful and thriving open-source project. To begin, clearly outline your goals for the project. Whether you aim to increase monthly active users (MAU), enhance the product, or expand the community, having a clear direction is crucial. Remember that an open-source project should truly embrace openness. It should be receptive to feedback, welcoming to contributions, and open to improvements.
